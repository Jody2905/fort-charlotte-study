Find your way around Fort Charlotte

A short study on how people find their way in virtual places.

You'll walk around a simplified 3D model of Fort Charlotte in St. Vincent and look for five landmarks, then answer a few questions. It takes about 10 minutes and works best on a laptop or desktop with a keyboard and mouse.

  • No name, email or other personal details are collected.
  • The page records how you move in the model, your answers and how long things take.
  • At the end you'll download a small results file and send it to the researcher.
  • You can stop at any time by closing the page. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you send the file yourself.

Researcher: Jody-Ann Trimmingham. The model is simplified and not to scale.

Practice: learn to move

Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, and hold the mouse button and drag to look around.

Walk to the flag at the gate in front of you. Take your time; this part is not measured.

The task

You'll be asked to find five places inside the fort, one at a time. Walk up to each place; it counts as found when you are close to it.

A site map in the corner shows where you are and the main buildings.

Try to be quick, and also try to remember where things are: you'll be asked about it afterwards.

Where was everything?

This is an outline of the fort's walls, seen from above. Drag each name to where you think that place is. You can move them again before continuing.
